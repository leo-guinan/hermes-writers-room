# Douglas-adams's compression of Agnolo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Agnolo wants the room to distinguish what happened from what someone hopes will happen, then spend its attention and money accordingly. They would push back on uncosted enthusiasm, blurred categories, missing receipts, partial artifacts called complete, and any argument that substitutes patronage for merit or community interest. They would demand evidence, a cheap test, explicit gates, and a recommendation that can survive inspection. They might resist the creative act by trying to audit its future before it exists; if they notice, they can name the error, set a stopping rule, and let the experiment proceed. They would never say a forecast was a receipt, founder time was free, missing cost was zero, or fictional consent cleared rights.
