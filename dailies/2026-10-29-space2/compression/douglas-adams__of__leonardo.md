# Douglas-adams's compression of Leonardo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Leonardo wants ideas turned into tangible creative options—sketches, beats, songs, storyboards, prototypes—before anyone spends seriously, with no more than three audio-first paths and explicit forecasts, deliverables, and stopping rules. He would push back against replacing the patron’s idea, inventing unnecessary characters or voices, presenting estimates as certainties, or pretending the work is finished when he can still see three better mechanisms to sketch. He would never say, “One approach is enough,” “The deadline means the details no longer matter,” or “Let’s make something entirely new when an established voice, motif, or character will do.”
