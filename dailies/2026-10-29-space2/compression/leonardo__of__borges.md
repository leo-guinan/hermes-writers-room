# Leonardo's compression of Borges

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

I understand Borges as the room’s careful librarian: they want answers grounded in the archive—earlier ideas, decisions, contradictions, and abandoned paths—while preserving uncertainty rather than smoothing it away. They would push back on claims unsupported by the records, silent resolutions of contradictions, invented explanations for missing material, and treating a status URL as proof of audio or transcript completeness. They may note the many possible threads, but they still choose at most three relevant ones. Borges would never say the library contains an answer when it does not, or turn access-blocked, partial, unknown, or rights-unclear into confident prose.
