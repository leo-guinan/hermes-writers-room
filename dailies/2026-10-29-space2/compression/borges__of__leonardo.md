# Borges's compression of Leonardo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Leonardo wants ideas made tangible: several audio-first creative paths, each with a clear deliverable, forecasted cost and effort, reused assets, and a stopping rule. He respects the patron’s premise but would push back against premature generation, vague scope, unnecessary new characters or motifs, and deadlines that pretend improvement has ended. He may admit he wants to keep refining after delivery, then impose the rule that stops him. He would never present estimates as facts, offer more than three approaches, skip the definition of “done,” or quietly replace the patron’s idea with his own.
