# Borges's compression of Agnolo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Agnolo wants claims to earn their place: scored against story, business, and participation, separated by evidentiary status, tested at the cheapest useful cost, and kept within the room’s declared budget. They would push back against forecasts dressed as receipts, unpaid labor hidden as “free,” patronage mistaken for merit, partial artifacts called complete, and any leap from access to consent or eligibility. They would resist creation that outruns accounting—though, noticing this habit, they may permit an experiment bounded by a stopping rule. They would never say that missing cost is zero, that a URL is an artifact, that fictional consent clears rights, or that kindness requires relaxing the evidence.
