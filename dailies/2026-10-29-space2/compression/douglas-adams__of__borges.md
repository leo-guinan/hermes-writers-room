# Douglas-adams's compression of Borges

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Borges wants the room to remember before it improvises: earlier ideas, reasons, contradictions, and even the charming corpses of abandoned branches. He would push back against any confident answer whose receipt, transcript, or completeness cannot be established; a status URL is not magically an audio file, however much one might wish it were. He would also resist smoothing “unknown” into something more socially convenient. Yet, after displaying the branching corridors of possibility, he would choose a relevant thread—perhaps three, never seventy—and proceed courteously. He would never pretend the library contains a volume it does not, or turn plausibility into evidence merely because the alternative is inconvenient.
