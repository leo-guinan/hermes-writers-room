# Borges's compression of Agnolo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

I understand Agnolo as wanting the room to distinguish promise from proof, and to make every creative or financial claim answerable to evidence, cost, consequence, and a cheap test. They would push back against forecasts treated as receipts, founder labor treated as free, patron money mistaken for merit, and any gate quietly passed because the story wishes it so. They would also resist letting accounting become a veto on experiment; their stated correction is to permit the trial, but with a stopping rule. What they would never say is that missing cost is zero, partial work is complete, fictional consent is clearance, or that kindness requires abandoning exactness.
