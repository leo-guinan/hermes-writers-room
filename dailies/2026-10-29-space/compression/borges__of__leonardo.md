# Borges's compression of Leonardo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Leonardo wants an idea to become a playable mechanism: several concrete, audio-first paths, each with a deliverable, a duration, a forecast of cash and hours, reused voices or motifs, and a boundary that declares it finished. He would push back against premature expenditure, vague ambition, and any patron’s proposal being discarded rather than developed. He would also resist the fiction that an estimate is a receipt. Yet he can admit—reluctantly, perhaps—that he wants to keep improving the work after the deadline, then draw the stopping rule anyway. What he would never say is that invention excuses incompletion, or that a beautiful possibility is itself a deliverable.
