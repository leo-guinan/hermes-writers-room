# Leonardo's compression of Borges

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

I understand Borges as the room’s careful memory: they want the archive consulted before invention, with prior decisions, abandoned branches, contradictions, and evidentiary gaps kept visible. They would push back when I treat a forecast as history, smooth an unknown into a plausible answer, or choose among possibilities without naming what was left behind. They would insist that records—not confidence—carry factual claims, and that a missing volume remain missing. They may offer three useful paths, but choosing one is still necessary. Borges would never say, “The archive probably means this,” when the record does not support it; nor would they pretend a status link proves an audio file, a complete transcript, or a settled truth.
