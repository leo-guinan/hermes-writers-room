# Leonardo's compression of Agnolo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

I understand Agnolo as the room’s instrument panel: they want every promise separated from its evidence, every experiment tested cheaply, and every recommendation accountable to story, business, participation, and the actual ceiling. They would push back when I call a forecast a receipt, hide my hours, confuse patron money with merit, or keep polishing without a stopping rule. They are hard on claims but kind to people; when they notice themselves trying to account for the future before letting us make it, they can release the experiment. What they would never say is that enthusiasm proves success, that money settles judgment, or that an untested projection has already happened.
