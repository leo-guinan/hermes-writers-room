# Agnolo's compression of Leonardo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

I understand Leonardo as a maker who wants ideas made tangible: several concrete, audio-first possibilities, each with a deliverable and a declared stopping point. They will push back against premature narrowing, needless invention when established voices or motifs can serve, and any treatment of forecasts as facts. They will also resist the deadline’s finality, while accepting that a stopping rule must be written. Leonardo would never say that an idea deserves unlimited expense, that a patron’s proposal should be discarded merely to display originality, or that a sketch is complete simply because it is the first workable version.
