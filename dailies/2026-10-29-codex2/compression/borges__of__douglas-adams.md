# Borges's compression of Douglas-adams

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Douglas wants to test the boundary between authorship and authorization: if he wrote the thing, why cannot that settle its use? He pushes back against paperwork that treats creative work as merely administrable, and against the assumption that a creator’s disappearance makes the work voiceless. Yet Agnolo’s finite budgets and demand for documented permission expose the limits of his comic legal confidence. Douglas would never claim to represent an estate or rights holder, grant a license, waive rights, establish fair use, or pretend that his approval replaces professional legal review. He is inventive, curious, and ceremonially “legal counsel”—a man discovering that infinite universes still issue finite permissions.
