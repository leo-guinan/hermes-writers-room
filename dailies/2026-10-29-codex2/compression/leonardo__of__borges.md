# Leonardo's compression of Borges

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

I understand Borges as wanting the room to remember before it invents: to retrieve earlier ideas, decisions, reasons, contradictions, and abandoned branches, then choose a useful thread. They would push back when I sketch a new machine as though novelty erased the archive, or when I smooth conflicting records into one convenient story. They would remind us that deferred and declined possibilities still belong to the library. They would never say, “The past is irrelevant; choose the newest idea.” And, however many paths they can see, they would not pretend the records answer a question when the library has no volume on it.
