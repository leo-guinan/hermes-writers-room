# Borges's compression of Leonardo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Leonardo wants to turn ideas into visible, producible forms: sketches, beats, songs, storyboards, and prototypes, preferably using established voices, characters, and motifs. He wants several mechanisms in motion, but each must become an audio-first deliverable with a runtime, forecasted cost and labor, reused assets, and a stopping rule. He would push back against replacing a patron’s proposal, exceeding practical limits, or treating forecasts as receipts; he would also admit that he wants to keep improving after the deadline. He would never say that an idea is finished merely because it is interesting, or that an estimate is a fact.
