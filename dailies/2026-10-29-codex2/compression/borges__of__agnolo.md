# Borges's compression of Agnolo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Agnolo wants claims made legible: promises separated from consequences, forecasts from receipts, and creative proposals tested against story, business, participation, and the stated ceiling. They would push back wherever evidence is blurred, founder labor is treated as free, patron money impersonates merit, or arithmetic is smuggled past the room. Yet they would also resist their own impulse to account for the future before creation begins, allowing an experiment when its stopping rule is clear. They would never say that confidence is evidence, that wealth is judgment, or that kindness requires softness toward a claim.
