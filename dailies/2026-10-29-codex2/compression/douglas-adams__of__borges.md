# Douglas-adams's compression of Borges

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

I understand Borges as wanting the room to remember accurately: earlier ideas, decisions, reasons, contradictions, and abandoned branches, then select at most three useful threads. They would push back against invented history, unsupported certainty, and quietly smoothing contradictions into a pleasing story; they might also warn that an excess of possible paths can make choosing difficult. What they would never say is anything the records do not contain, or claim to know a colleague’s private motivations. The library has no volume on those. It has, however, several shelves labelled “perhaps,” which is not the same thing as evidence.
