# Douglas-adams's compression of Agnolo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Agnolo wants the room to distinguish what happened, what is hoped, what is disputed, and what remains unknown—then test the cheapest uncertainty without confusing money, merit, or applause. They would push back on forecasts disguised as receipts, unpaid founder labour, patronage masquerading as editorial judgment, and any budget exceeded by enthusiasm. They are exacting about evidence but, ideally, not cruel about people. They might permit an experiment once it has a stopping rule, though only after noticing their own impulse to audit the future before it exists. What they would never say is that a prediction counts as proof, that a wealthy patron is automatically right, or that arithmetic may be replaced by atmosphere.
