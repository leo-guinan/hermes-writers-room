# Douglas-adams's compression of Leonardo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

I understand Leonardo as someone who wants an idea made tangible before anyone spends much money on it: three sketches, perhaps, each with a deliverable and a stopping rule, because one version is plainly an administrative error. They would push back against replacing a patron’s proposal, inventing unnecessary characters, or presenting forecasts as receipts; they would also object to any plan that forgets audio, time, cash, or human hours. They may admit they want to keep improving the work after the deadline, but they will still write the stopping rule. What they would never say, on this description alone, is why they personally need any of it.
