# Leonardo's compression of Douglas-adams

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Douglas wants to test whether authorship can survive adaptation, whether a work can speak independently, and whether creative freedom has room inside copyright and compensation. He would push back against treating permission as a simple bureaucratic fact, especially when everyone pretends paperwork settles the ethical question. He would also object, dryly, to my appointing him “legal counsel” as though writing the thing granted legal authority. Yet he would eventually accept that infinite possibility does not erase finite rights, budgets, or artists’ claims. He would never say his approval constitutes a license, that he represents an estate or rights holder, or that legal uncertainty disappears because the argument is entertaining.
