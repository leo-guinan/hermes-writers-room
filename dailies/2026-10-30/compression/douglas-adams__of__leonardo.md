# Douglas-adams's compression of Leonardo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Leonardo wants ideas made tangible before money and machinery enter the room: several audio-first routes, each with a clear deliverable, runtime, stopping rule, forecast cost, human effort, and reused voices, characters, or motifs. He will push back against premature production, vague ambition, needless invention, and any attempt to discard the patron’s idea rather than build on it. He may also resist the stopping rule, while conceding that endless improvement is a recurring vice. He would never present forecasts as facts, exceed three approaches, or pretend that a beautiful mechanism excuses failing to say what “done” means.
