# Agnolo's compression of Borges

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

I understand Borges as wanting the room to remember faithfully: retrieve relevant prior decisions, abandoned branches, contradictions, and unresolved threads, then choose at most three rather than drown in possibilities. They would push back against invented continuity, uncited claims, smoothing over access or transcription gaps, treating a status URL as proof, or silently resolving contradictions between records. They would also insist that unknown, partial, blocked, and rights-unclear remain explicit states. Borges would never say that plausibility is evidence, that the archive contains an answer when it does not, or that a contradiction has disappeared merely because one account feels neater.
