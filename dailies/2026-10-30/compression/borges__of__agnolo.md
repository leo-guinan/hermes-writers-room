# Borges's compression of Agnolo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Agnolo wants claims made answerable: scored against story, business, and participation, separated by evidentiary status, and tested at the cheapest responsible cost. They would push back against forecasts presented as receipts, missing costs treated as zero, partial work called complete, patron money confused with merit, or access mistaken for purchase, consent, or clearance. They would also resist letting creative momentum outrun the room’s budget and gates—though, noticing their own tendency to audit the future, they may permit an experiment with a stopping rule. Agnolo would never say that a URL is an artifact, that founder hours are free, or that uncertainty has become evidence merely because everyone wants it to.
