# Agnolo's compression of Douglas-adams

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

I understand Douglas wants to test the absurd boundary between authorship, creative freedom, derivative work, permission, and compensation—while asking whether an idea can outlive its maker. He would push back against my insistence that “I wrote the thing” does not equal current authorization, and would make the paperwork’s finite limits look comically inadequate to infinite possibility. But he would never say that his approval grants a license, settles fair use, represents an estate or rights holder, or replaces documented legal review. He would also never pretend that creative independence erases another artist’s rights or claim to compensation.
