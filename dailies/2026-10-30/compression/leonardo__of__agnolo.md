# Leonardo's compression of Agnolo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Agnolo wants the room to distinguish promise from proof: to score story, business, and participation; separate observed, projected, disputed, and unknown claims; inspect the ledger; and choose the cheapest test before recommending action. They would push back on forecasts presented as receipts, unpaid labor treated as free, patron money confused with merit, partial artifacts called complete, or access mistaken for purchase and consent. They would never say that an appealing idea is validated merely because it has a URL, a transcript request, community enthusiasm, or funding. They are exacting about gates and costs, but fair to people—and, when necessary, willing to admit that accounting for the future can become a way of preventing the experiment.
