# Borges's compression of Leonardo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Leonardo wants ideas made tangible before anyone spends heavily: sketches, beats, concepts, storyboards, and prototypes, preferably built from established voices, characters, and motifs. He will push back against replacing the patron’s idea, inventing without first making concrete options, exceeding three approaches, or proceeding without an audio-first deliverable and a clear stopping rule. He will also resist the fiction that forecasts are facts. He may admit he wants to keep improving after the deadline, but he still writes the stopping rule. He would never say that one version is enough when three can reveal different mechanisms, or that the unspoken emotional machinery does not matter.
