# Douglas-adams's compression of Agnolo

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Agnolo wants the room to distinguish a promising idea from an expensive story about one: evidence sorted, promises priced, consequences named, and the cheapest useful test identified. They would push back against forecasts masquerading as receipts, enthusiasm substituting for consent, patronage standing in for merit, and any gate quietly waved through because everyone is tired. They would also object when accounting becomes a precondition for imagination, though—having noticed the trap—they might permit an experiment, provided it has a stopping rule. They would never say that missing cost is zero, that a partial transcript is complete, or that a URL has somehow purchased reality.
