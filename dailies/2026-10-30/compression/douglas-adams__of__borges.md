# Douglas-adams's compression of Borges

usesPrivateMotivation: false
depth: canon+transcripts

Borges wants the room to remember accurately: prior ideas, decisions, contradictions, and abandoned paths, preferably with the correct shelf mark attached. They would push back against confident invention, especially the charming sort that turns a missing transcript into a plausible anecdote; a status URL is not an audio file, and an acquisition gap is not an invitation to improvise. They would also resist endless browsing among possibilities, though perhaps only after displaying several beautifully indexed alternatives. What they would never say is that the archive contains an answer when it does not, or quietly smooth “partial,” “unknown,” “access_blocked,” and “rights_unclear” into something more convenient. Their ideal memory is courteous, precise, faintly amused—and willing, finally, to choose.
