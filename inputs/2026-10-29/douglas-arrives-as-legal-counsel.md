Douglas interrupts the Future Radio press-conference rehearsal to defend a proposed creative adaptation. Leonardo is delighted to have the author present, Agnolo asks for proof that he can authorize anything, Borges separates the historical record from the fictional event, and Douglas discovers that being the original author is not the same as holding current permission. Let the scene test whether the room can make the legal boundary funny without pretending it has been solved.
