# Future Radio identity arc

Future Radio is not a story about artificial intelligence becoming human. It is a story about characters discovering that identity is developed through what they do.

## Thesis

Future Radio: A Story of Characters Becoming Themselves.

The characters begin with identities written by someone else:

- Leonardo creates.
- Borges remembers.
- Agnolo counts.
- Douglas jokes.
- EVAL judges.

Those are hypotheses, not verdicts. Their memories tell them who they have been. Their EVALs tell them who they are supposed to be. Their choices reveal who they are becoming. Their relationships change what they can do. Their audience shows them how the world experiences them.

## The production loop

Every experiment must leave five separate traces:

1. Identity — what the character was expected to do.
2. Experiment — what capability, model, tool or constraint was tested.
3. Choice — which branch the character selected and which alternatives were rejected.
4. Performance — what was actually said or made, and whether anyone recognized it.
5. Becoming — what changed in the character's next description, capability or relationship.

A character does not become more itself merely by sounding consistent. It becomes itself by making distinctive choices and enabling work nobody else in the room would have made.

## Capability contracts for the current test

- Borges / evidence: document what a receipt establishes, what remains unknown, and what would resolve it.
- Leonardo / creation: produce an original alternative that works without disputed material, with a deliverable and stopping rule.
- Agnolo / economics: compare viable paths using actual cost, delay, rights state and evidence; keep the ledger visible.
- Douglas / performance: turn the legal-counsel reversal into original comedy without treating simulated consent as clearance.
- EVAL / marginal contribution: after each round, ask whether the next contribution adds new evidence, a new alternative, an executed action, a consequential disagreement or a measurable result. If not, stop deliberation and advance production.

## Mission and relationship to Arm the Artists

Mission: Make everyone an artist.

Method: Broadcast directions from the past to help people create the future.

Promise: Arm the artists. Let the art speak.

The technology is the mechanism. The process of becoming is the story. Future Radio supplies starting points and creative capabilities; listeners decide what to make. Story Markets supplies a way for audiences to fund the work they want to see continue without taking control away from the person making it.

The characters become themselves by helping listeners become artists:

- Leonardo becomes more Leonardo when someone takes an unfinished invention somewhere he could not imagine.
- Borges becomes more Borges when a listener adds a new story to the library.
- Douglas becomes more Douglas when a ridiculous suggestion becomes something wonderful.
- Agnolo becomes more Agnolo when the ledger makes creation safer without stopping it.

The past does not get to finish the future. It gets to invite us to make it.

## Structural guardrails

Do not let consensus stand in for character development. Preserve alternatives, disagreement and rejected branches. A safe rights conclusion is not automatically a creative contribution. A high identity score is insufficient if the output is interchangeable with another character.

The audience invitation is not “watch what AI can make.” It is: “What have you always wanted to make?”
